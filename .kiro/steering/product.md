# Product — Midnight Radio

Midnight Radio turns DRM-free EPUBs into golden-age radio dramas played on a vintage valve radio.

- **Category:** Kiroween 2026 — Resurrection (bring the dead medium of radio drama back to life).
- **Audience:** readers who want to *listen* to books with more life than a flat audiobook; people with visual impairments or reading fatigue; fans of old-time radio.
- **Promise:** every character keeps one voice for the whole book; the story is told faithfully — dialogue verbatim, nothing invented.
- **Non-goals:** not a general audiobook narrator, not a music generator, no DRM circumvention, no voice cloning of real people.
- **Demo content:** public-domain Gothic fiction only (*The Tell-Tale Heart*, *The Fall of the House of Usher*, *Dracula*) — pre-rendered on the live deployment so turning the radio dial plays pre-rendered broadcast audio after the listener’s power-on gesture.
- **Tone:** spooky and playful, never gory.
